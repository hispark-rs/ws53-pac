# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1

- Introduce an experimental WS53 application-core PAC generated from the
  SystemRDL source revision recorded in `generation.json`.
- Include typed register and interrupt definitions, RISC-V `device.x`, and
  host-side register-layout and access-contract tests.
- Hardware coverage remains incomplete. Access semantics and public names may
  change as the remaining device and dual-core behavior is verified.
